About the Candidate

Rooted in Connecticut.
Fighting for All of Us.

"Fairfield County deserves a representative who shows up — at town halls, on the job site, in the hospital, and in Washington."

Jay has spent his career at the intersection of community advocacy and public service in Connecticut's 4th District — from Bridgeport to Greenwich, Stamford to Norwalk.

A graduate of local public schools, Jay knows firsthand what working families in this district face: rising housing costs, stagnant wages, and healthcare that's out of reach. He's ready to bring that perspective to Washington.

Jay has spent over a decade in community organizing, labor advocacy, and local government — building coalitions across every corner of our district.

The Platform

What Jay Will Fight For

A bold, practical agenda grounded in the real challenges facing Fairfield County families — with clear plans to deliver results.

🏠

Affordable Housing

Expand federal housing vouchers, crack down on corporate landlord speculation, and fund construction of workforce housing across CT-4.

🏥

Healthcare for All

Protect and expand the ACA, lower prescription drug costs, and fight for a public option so no CT family goes bankrupt from a medical bill.

🌿

Clean Energy Future

Invest in offshore wind, solar, and clean transportation — creating good-paying union jobs right here in Connecticut while tackling the climate crisis.

📚

Education & Opportunity

Fully fund public schools, make community college free, and expand job training so every student in our district has a path to success.

⚖️

Economic Fairness

Raise the federal minimum wage, strengthen worker organizing rights, and make the ultra-wealthy pay their fair share in taxes.

🛡️

Public Safety

Invest in community policing, mental health crisis response, and common-sense gun safety legislation that protects our families.

Our District

CT's 4th Congressional District

Spanning the communities of southwestern Connecticut — from urban Bridgeport to coastal Greenwich — the 4th District is one of the most economically diverse in New England.

That diversity is our strength. Jay will represent every community: the factory worker in Bridgeport, the teacher in Norwalk, the small business owner in Stamford, and the family in Shelton.
